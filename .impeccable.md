## Design Context

### Users

DSH users who keep several conversation windows active at once and need to understand, connect, and coordinate those windows without leaving the current conversation.

### Brand Personality

Native, compact, calm. The control should feel like part of DSH rather than a separate technical tool, and connection state should be clear without competing with the conversation title.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use DSH design tokens, restrained geometry, and both light and dark themes. Prefer one quiet icon in the header with progressive disclosure. Show the relationship inside a stable anchored panel instead of drawing lines across the reorderable sidebar. Avoid long engineering labels, decorative gradients, and oversized cards.

### Design Principles

- Show who is connected, not only how many connections exist.
- Keep the resting state compact and reveal management details on demand.
- Represent one-to-many relationships in a contained tree that survives sidebar reorder, search, and scrolling.
- Use plain action words such as "Invite another window" and preserve visible copy feedback.
- Keep keyboard navigation, reduced motion, light theme, and dark theme behavior complete.
